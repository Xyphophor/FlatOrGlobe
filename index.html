This site compares the flat-plane and globe models of the Earth against measurable evidence: horizon dip, hidden heights, elevation angles, refraction, star trails, seismic travel times, gravity, magnetism, eclipses, GPS, aviation and more. Every tab states in advance what result would vindicate each model, gives the flat model its best case, and shows real data with numbered sources.

The pages are drawn by JavaScript. The same content is published as plain text: llms.txt (index and method), llms-full.txt (everything in one file), and one Markdown file per tab under /ai/, for example results and what would refute each model.
