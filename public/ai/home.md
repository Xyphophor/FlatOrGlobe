*Twenty measurements · one shape, one motion*

Each card below is one thing you can do, or check, without buying anything. No explanation here on purpose: the tab behind each card carries the geometry, the sources and the numbers, and computes both models side by side. They come in two families. The first asks what shape and size the ground has; one radius, R ≈ 6,371 km, satisfies all of them at once, and no flat arrangement satisfies two. The second asks how the ground moves: it turns once in 23 h 56 min about an axis tilted to each place's vertical, and goes round the Sun once a year.

## How to read the labels

Each section that shows evidence carries one of three labels. The rule is strict: a distance in kilometres taken from coordinates, a map or a terrain model already contains the Earth's radius, and so do satellite orbits (WGS84), the GRS80 reference gravity, the IGRF magnetic model and any speed that was itself measured with globe distances. Latitudes and longitudes, taken as angles, do not. A measurement that touched the radius once stays a check, even if the radius was removed again later.

- [measurement] the curvature or the radius comes out of data the radius never entered.
- [check] real data compared with the globe, or produced with a globe model at some point.
- [prediction] what a model predicts, nothing measured.

Sections without a label explain, tell history, answer objections or give the flat model its best case.

## Motions: the rotation and the orbit

Instruments that feel the Earth turn, and light that shows it moving through the year. A plane at rest predicts none of it; a disc spinning about its pole predicts the same rotation everywhere.

- [Rotation](/ai/rot.md) — [measurement] [prediction] Read the Earth's rotation from ring lasers and falling balls, and see why the latitude dependence rules out both flat models.

- [Orbit](/ai/orb.md) — [measurement] [check] [prediction] Size the Earth's orbit from aberration, light time and the Doppler shift of the Sun and of τ Ceti.

- [Satellites](/ai/sat.md) — [measurement] Fit 79 satellites' orbits, 80 receivers, the Earth's mass and rotation to one day of Doppler, starting from a flat map.

- [Scale](/ai/scale.md) — See the Earth's motions on one clock, each hand at its true speed, and the distances from the Moon to Andromeda.

## Measure it together

One point cannot reveal a sphere: its radius needs positions far apart. These modules are neither demonstrations nor tools. Each person measures alone, in their own browser, and the site stores and sends nothing; then people thousands of kilometres apart exchange their results by hand and check that they find the same radius.

- [Your own GPS](/ai/rx.md) — [check] [prediction] Record your phone's raw satellite signals, solve your own position, then measure the Earth's radius with people far away.
